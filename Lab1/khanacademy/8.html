Travel to Thailand

Info about Thailand

Thailand,officially the Kingdom of Thailand,and formerly known as Siam until 1939, is a country located in mainland Southeast Asia. It shares land borders with Myanmar to the west and northwest, Laos to the east and northeast, Cambodia to the southeast, and Malaysia to the south.

The most popular places

  1. Bangkok
  2. Phuket
  3. Pattaya

Reasons to visit Thailand

  • Beautiful nature and beaches Thailand is famous for its stunning beaches, clear blue sea, tropical islands, jungles, and waterfalls. It is a perfect place to relax and enjoy nature.
  • Rich culture and delicious food Thailand has a unique culture with beautiful temples, traditions, and festivals. Thai food is very popular all over the world and is known for its amazing flavors.

Thailand's climate is influenced by monsoon winds that have a seasonal character (the southwest and northeast monsoon).[134]: 2  Most of the country is classified as Köppen's tropical savanna climate.[135] The majority of the south, as well as the eastern tip, have a tropical monsoon climate. Parts of the south also have a tropical rainforest climate.
